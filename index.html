About

I am a PhD candidate at the Causal Learning and Artificial Intelligence Lab at IST Austria, supervised by Francesco Locatello. My research focuses on representation learning, in particular the geometry and structure of neural representations. I am interested in how representations are organized, when they collapse, and how they behave under distribution shift. More broadly, my work spans self-supervised learning, generalization, and generative modeling.

I am co-affiliated with the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative's AI for Science program, which supports my PhD research.

Prior to beginning my PhD, I earned a master's degree from Sabanci University. During this time, I was a member of the VPA Lab and was under the supervision of Asst. Prof. Huseyin Ozkan and Asst. Prof. Erchan Aptoula. The primary focus of my master's studies was on group activity recognition and domain generalization.

In the summer of 2019, I completed a summer internship at Erasmus University Rotterdam.
