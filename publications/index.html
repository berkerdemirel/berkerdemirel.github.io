  • λ-JEPA: Spectral Anti-Collapse Regularization for Self-Supervised Learning

    Berker Demirel*, Clémentine Dominé*, Valentino Maiorca, Marco Fumero, Marco Mondelli, Francesco Locatello

  • Assessing Sample Quality in Conditional Generation under Compositional Shift

  • Out-of-Distribution Detection with Relative Angles

    Berker Demirel, Marco Fumero, Francesco Locatello

    Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems · NeurIPS 2025

    ORA scores a feature by the angle between it and its projection onto the decision boundaries, measured from the mean of in-distribution features. It is model-agnostic, hyperparameter-free, scale-invariant, and lets scores from several models be summed.

  • Adjusting Pretrained Backbones for Performativity

    Berker Demirel, Lingjing Kong, Kun Zhang, Theofanis Karaletsos, Celestine Mendler-Dünner, Francesco Locatello

    arXiv:2410.04499 · Preprint

    A modular approach to performative label shift for pretrained backbones: it adapts a model before the shift and anticipates how different models would shift the distribution, enabling informed model selection and zero-shot transfer across backbone updates.
